Weekend / holiday adjustment — These periods are counted backward from the return date. When a 16-, 8-, 28- or 10-day deadline lands on a Saturday, Sunday, legal holiday or court recess day, it has been moved back to the preceding court day, so the full period is preserved. The 4-day reply period is under 7 days, so under R. 1:3-1 it is counted in court days, excluding Saturdays, Sundays and legal holidays.
Holiday schedule not yet confirmed. The Supreme Court's legal-holiday and court-recess orders built into this calculator run through June 30, 2027. For later dates the calculator uses projected holidays: federal holidays, Good Friday and Election Day. Recess days such as the Judicial College or year-end recess are not included. Check the court's order for that court year before relying on these dates.
Assumes e-filing (eCourts) or hand delivery. These dates do not account for service by mail. Under R. 1:6-3(c), service of motion papers is complete only on receipt at adverse counsel's office or a self-represented party's address, and receipt by ordinary mail is presumed on the third business day after mailing. If papers will be mailed, mail them early enough to be received by the deadline shown.

Not legal advice. This calculator is a convenience tool. It applies R. 1:6-3(a), R. 4:46-1, R. 1:3-1 and the New Jersey Judiciary's published holiday and recess schedule as programmed, and it does not account for judge- or vicinage-specific practices, scheduling orders, adjournments, or relaxation of time by the court. Always verify deadlines independently against the Rules of Court, any applicable orders, and the Judiciary's official calendar.
